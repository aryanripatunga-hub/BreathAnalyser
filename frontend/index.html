Our Technology

How BreathAnalysis Works

Our three-step process transforms raw breath sounds into actionable health insights.

01

Upload Audio

Record or upload a breath sound in .wav, .mp3, or other common audio formats. The system accepts recordings up to 20 seconds.

02

AI Analysis

Our ResNet-18 deep learning model converts the audio into a mel spectrogram and analyzes frequency patterns characteristic of different respiratory conditions.

03

Get Results

View detailed confidence scores, interactive charts, spectrogram visualizations, and comprehensive information about detected conditions — all in seconds.

R18

ResNet-18

18-layer deep residual network pre-trained on ImageNet, fine-tuned on respiratory spectrograms.

DB

ICBHI 2017

Trained on the ICBHI Respiratory Sound Database — the gold standard benchmark for lung sound classification.

93.7%

93.7% Accuracy

Achieves 93.7% overall accuracy across 4 classes with class-balanced correction for reliable predictions.

RT

Real-time

Analysis completes in seconds, providing instant feedback with spectrogram and waveform visualizations.
